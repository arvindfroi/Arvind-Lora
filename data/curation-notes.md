## Tier system

- **gold** — verbatim, spontaneous, unassisted writing. Train on this freely. The real
  fingerprint: typos, dialect, smileys, bluntness.
- **silver** — text Arvind sent under his name but likely AI-drafted and human-edited
  (Fedora/PPSSPP/Anthropic mail, exam notes possibly organized with AI). Use sparingly
  (≤20% of training mix) and only for the registers they represent; they define his
  *edited-polished* voice.
- **bronze** — uncertain authorship (group assignments, heavily AI-assisted docs).
  Reference only; exclude from training by default.

## Excluded entirely

- Emails literally written by Claude and sent from his account (e.g. the 2026-05-18
  "Modded Smash Bros Ultimate setup" mail opens "Hei Emil, Claude here").
- Forwards, one-word/attachment-only mails, self-notes containing only links or keys,
  unsubscribe autogenerated mail.
- The Syncthing/device-key self-emails (secrets, zero style value).
- The KSI membership mail (only personal data, no prose).
- Drive docs that are clearly AI research output (e.g. "Decentralized Architectures
  for LLM Inference…").
- Group-assignment sections attributable to other students (ORG219 sections labeled
  with other names).

## Redactions applied

- Third-party full names in sensitive contexts → `[NAVN]`.
- Arvind's phone number → `[TELEFON]`.
- Message text from counterparties is never included — only Arvind's own words.
